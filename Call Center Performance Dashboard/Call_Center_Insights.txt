
Call Center Dashboard – Insights & Summary

1. Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides a complete overview of call center performance, analyzing call volume, call duration, response time, customer sentiment, issue reasons, and agent performance across different states and channels.
It consists of two main report pages:
- Home View (Summary & Visual Insights)
- Grid View (Detailed Call-Level Records)

2. Key KPIs
- Total Calls: 32.9K
- Total Duration in Hours: 13.74K hours
- Total Call Duration in Minutes: 824.2K mins
- Average Call Duration: 25.02 minutes
- Response Time in %: 75.3%

3. Call Volume Insights
- Highest calls are observed on Fridays and Thursdays.
- Weekends (Saturday & Sunday) show lower call activity.
- Los Angeles, Baltimore, and Chicago are the top call center cities with the most call volume.

4. Call Reason Insights
- Billing Questions account for the highest number of calls (23.5K).
- Payments and Service Outages follow with smaller volumes.
This shows that a majority of customers reach out for billing-related issues.

5. Sentiment Analysis
- Negative and Neutral sentiments dominate call interactions.
- Very Positive responses remain the lowest, indicating customer frustration in certain areas.

6. Channel Breakdown
- Call-Center (Voice) accounts for 32% of total calls.
- Chatbot and Email channels follow at 25% and 23%.
- Web calls contribute the least (20%).
This reflects diverse interaction channels with a strong focus on voice communication.

7. State-Level Performance
- Highest number of calls originate from the United States region mapped on the dashboard.
- State-based insights help managers understand where customer service demand is the greatest.

8. Grid View Insights
- Contains detailed call-level data including:
  - Caller ID
  - City
  - Customer Name
  - Response Time Category (Within SLA, Above SLA, Below SLA)
  - Channel
  - State
  - Call Reason
  - Total Call Duration (minutes)
This view is ideal for quality audits, SLA monitoring, and performance reviews.

9. SLA Performance
- Majority of interactions fall within SLA, but a notable number fall Above or Below SLA, indicating improvement opportunities in handling times.

10. Summary
This dashboard provides a strong analytical view of call center operations, enabling:
- Better staffing decisions based on call patterns
- Improved SLA and quality monitoring
- Insightful understanding of customer issues and sentiment
- Data-driven decision-making for channel optimization
