# Sales Performance, Effort Efficiency & Pipeline Risk Dashboard 📊

## Overview
This project provides a **360° view of sales performance, effort utilization, and pipeline risk**.  
The dashboard focuses on understanding revenue health, time efficiency, deal progression, and conversion risks to support better strategic decision-making.

---

## Objectives
- Evaluate overall sales and revenue performance
- Measure efficiency of time and effort spent on deals
- Identify pipeline bottlenecks and conversion risks
- Highlight improvement areas using data-driven insights

---

## Key KPIs
- **Total Deal Value:** 165M INR  
- **Total Deals:** 23  
- **Won Deals:** 2  
- **Win Rate:** 8.7%  
- **Revenue per Hour:** 238.10K INR  
- **Average Deal Value:** 7.18M INR  
- **Total Hours Spent:** 693.65

---

## Key Insights
- High deal value exists, but conversion rate is very low.
- Majority of deals are stuck in early pipeline stages.
- Effort is high, but not proportionally translating into wins.
- Strong revenue per hour indicates potential if conversion improves.
- Pipeline congestion is the biggest revenue risk.

---

## Business Recommendations
- Shift focus from lead volume to deal closure.
- Improve qualification before Commercial Review stage.
- Reallocate effort from low-probability deals.
- Monitor stage-wise conversion metrics closely.
- Focus senior resources on high-value late-stage deals.
