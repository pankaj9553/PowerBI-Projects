INSURANCE ATTACH PERFORMANCE – KEY INSIGHTS
===========================================

1. Overall Performance Summary
------------------------------
- The overall Average Insurance Attach Rate is 18%, indicating low overall adoption.
- The Maximum Attach Rate reaches 100%, proving that full attachment is achievable at store level.
- Month-over-Month (MoM) growth shows a slight positive increase of 0.1%, indicating stable but slow improvement.

2. Branch-Level Performance
---------------------------
- Pune is the top-performing branch with an average attach rate of 27.7%.
- Delhi NCR follows closely at 24.4%, showing strong potential.
- Mumbai (17.3%) and Thane (14.9%) fall near the overall average.
- Gujarat (13.5%) and Telangana (11.8%) are underperforming and require focused interventions.

3. Store-Level Insights
-----------------------
- Top-performing stores consistently maintain attach rates above 50%, indicating strong sales execution.
- Several stores fall under the 'Low Attach' category, pulling down overall performance.
- High variation within the same branch suggests that performance depends more on store practices than region.

4. Monthly Trend Analysis
-------------------------
- Insurance attach rate has steadily improved from August to December.
- The sharpest improvement is observed between October and November.
- December maintains the peak level, indicating sustained improvement rather than a temporary spike.

5. Risk & Opportunity Areas
----------------------------
- Stores with attach rates below 10% represent immediate improvement opportunities.
- Medium-performing stores (15–25%) can be converted into high performers through training and incentives.
- Consistency across months remains a challenge for several stores.

6. Actionable Recommendations
------------------------------
- Replicate best practices from top-performing Pune stores across other branches.
- Introduce targeted training programs for low-attach stores.
- Track store-level performance weekly to reduce volatility.
- Align sales incentives with insurance attachment to drive behavioral change.

Conclusion
----------
While overall insurance attachment remains low, the presence of high-performing stores and a positive monthly trend indicate strong growth potential. Focused store-level interventions and replication of best practices can significantly improve overall attach performance.
