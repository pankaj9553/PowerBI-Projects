
# Insurance Attach Performance Dashboard 📊

## Overview
This project analyzes **Insurance Attach Performance** across multiple branches and stores to understand adoption trends, identify high and low performers, and uncover actionable improvement opportunities.  
The dashboard is designed to support **business decision-making** through clear KPIs, trends, and store-level insights.

---

## Objective
- Measure overall insurance attachment performance
- Compare performance across branches and stores
- Track monthly trends and momentum
- Identify underperforming areas and growth opportunities
- Provide data-driven recommendations for improvement

---

## Key KPIs
- **Average Attach Rate:** 18%
- **Maximum Attach Rate:** 100%
- **Month-over-Month Change:** +0.1%
- **Top Performing Branch:** Pune (27.7%)

---

## Dashboard Components
- KPI Cards: Avg Attach %, Max Attach %, MoM Change
- Branch Performance Bar Chart: Avg Attach % by branch
- Top 5 Stores: Highest average attach rates
- Monthly Trend Line: Insurance attach trend from Aug–Dec
- Detail Grid: Store-level attach %, branch, and category
- Store x Month Matrix: Monthly attach % by store

---

## Key Insights
- Insurance attachment is relatively low overall, indicating strong improvement potential.
- Large performance variation exists between stores within the same branch.
- Pune and Delhi NCR demonstrate strong sales execution and best practices.
- A steady upward trend is observed from August to December.
- Several stores remain in the low-attach category (<10%) and need immediate attention.

---

## Business Recommendations
- Replicate best practices from high-performing stores across regions.
- Provide focused training for low-attach stores.
- Align sales incentives with insurance attachment goals.
- Track store-level performance weekly to improve consistency.
