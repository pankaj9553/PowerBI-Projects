Electric Vehicle Analysis – Insights

1. Overall Adoption
- Total registered electric vehicles: 150.48K
- The average electric range across all vehicles is 67.88 KM

2. BEV vs PHEV Distribution
- BEV Vehicles: 117K (77.6% of total EVs)
- PHEV Vehicles: 34K (22.4% of total EVs)
BEVs dominate the market, indicating a strong shift toward fully electric mobility.

3. Growth Trend by Model Year
- Steady increase from 2010 to 2017.
- Major spike after 2018 with peak in 2023 (37K vehicles).
- Slight dip after 2023, likely due to partial data.
EV adoption surged sharply in the last five years.

4. Top Manufacturers
- TESLA leads with 69K vehicles.
- Other major contributors: Nissan (13K), Chevrolet (12K), Ford (8K), BMW (6K), KIA (6K).
Tesla is the main driver of EV adoption.

5. Popular EV Models
- Leading models: Model Y, Model 3.
- Other popular models include LEAF, BOLT EV, VOLT, Model X, Model S.

6. Geographic Distribution
- High EV adoption in California, Washington, Texas, New York.
- Lower adoption in central and southern regions.
Coastal states show stronger EV penetration.

7. CAFV Eligibility
- 46% vehicles are CAFV eligible.
Nearly half the EVs benefit from clean-fuel incentives.

Conclusion
EV adoption in the US is rising rapidly, driven by Tesla and coastal states. BEVs dominate the market, reflecting trust in full-electric mobility. The surge from 2018–2023 highlights advancements in range, infrastructure, and incentives.
