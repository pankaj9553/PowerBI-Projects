# 📊 Electric Vehicle Analysis Dashboard – Power BI

A comprehensive **Power BI dashboard** analyzing the adoption, distribution, and trends of Electric Vehicles (EVs) across the United States. This project highlights key insights into EV growth, leading manufacturers, popular models, and geographic patterns.

## 🚗 Project Overview

This dashboard analyzes more than **150K electric vehicles**, both **BEVs (Battery Electric Vehicles)** and **PHEVs (Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles)**.  
It helps understand:

- EV adoption trends by year  
- Top manufacturers  
- Popular EV models  
- State-wise EV distribution  
- CAFV (Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle) eligibility  
- BEV vs PHEV comparison  
- Average electric range  

---

## 📈 Key Insights

### 🔹 1. Overall Adoption
- Total EVs: **150.48K**  
- Average electric range: **67.88 KM**

### 🔹 2. BEV vs PHEV Market Share
- **BEV:** 117K (**77.6%**)  
- **PHEV:** 34K (**22.4%**)  
➡ Strong shift toward full-electric vehicles.

### 🔹 3. Growth Trend by Model Year
- Rapid growth after **2018**  
- Peak EV adoption in **2023 (37K vehicles)**  
- Indicates continuous improvement in charging infrastructure and government incentives

### 🔹 4. Top Manufacturers
- **Tesla – 69K**  
- Nissan – 13K  
- Chevrolet – 12K  
- Ford – 8K  
- BMW – 6K  
- KIA – 6K  

### 🔹 5. Most Popular Models
- **Model Y**  
- **Model 3**  
- Nissan Leaf  
- Bolt EV / Volt  
- Model X / Model S

### 🔹 6. Geographic Distribution
- Highest EV adoption in:  
  - **California**  
  - **Washington**  
  - **Texas**  
  - **New York**  
➡ Coastal states show strong EV penetration.

### 🔹 7. CAFV Eligibility
- **46%** of EVs are eligible under CAFV incentive programs.  
➡ Shows strong participation in clean-fuel benefits.
